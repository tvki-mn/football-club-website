Our History

Building communities through football since 1973.

The Beginning

Mungkas FC was founded in 1973 by a group of Bougainvillean and Solomon Island students attending the University of Papua New Guinea. The club was led by senior statesman Paul Nerau, whose vision laid the foundation for a club that continues to serve its community today.

Our Journey

1973

Mungkas FC was established at the University of Papua New Guinea.

1980s

The club adopted the name SOBOU, representing Solomon and Bougainville.

1990s

Following events in Bougainville and Solomon Islands, the club returned to its original name, Mungkas FC.

Today

Mungkas FC continues to develop football while creating opportunities for young people and strengthening the community.

Our Purpose

Aim

Through football, the club seeks to educate, support and inspire youth, promoting peace and creating a better society for future generations.

Mission

Empowering communities through football, fostering social change and nurturing personal growth.

Vision

To be a leading football club that brings players, families and communities together to build a stronger and healthier society.
